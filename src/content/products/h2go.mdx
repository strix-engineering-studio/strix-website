## Product direction

H2Go is designed for water-can suppliers and delivery businesses that need a clearer operating system for recurring customer orders and daily fulfilment.

## Product capabilities

- Customer and subscription records
- Order and delivery management
- Delivery operations and route planning
- Day-to-day business visibility

**Product status:** In development.
