## Product direction

Imrabo explores an AI assistant and AI-first operating experience designed to bring tasks, context and connected workflows into one product ecosystem.

## Product surfaces

- **Website:** [imrabo.com](https://imrabo.com/)
- **Workspace:** [app.imrabo.com](https://app.imrabo.com/log-in)
- **Mobile application:** Flutter-based Android application in the Imrabo product ecosystem. A public app-store listing has not been confirmed, so this page does not link to an unverified download.

## Areas of exploration

- Context-aware assistance
- Workflow and task orchestration
- Structured tool invocation
- A unified user experience across tasks
- Connected web and mobile experiences

**Product status:** Prototype and iterative development.
