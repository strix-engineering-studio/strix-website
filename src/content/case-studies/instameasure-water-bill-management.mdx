## Product context

The application needed to support customers, delegates, administrators, and super administrators without requiring four separate mobile applications.

## Engineering delivered

- A shared Flutter application for all four user roles
- Role-aware workflows and permission-aware interfaces
- Firebase integration for application data and backend services
- A single application codebase serving customer, delegate, admin, and super-admin experiences

## Outcome

Delivered a water-bill management application supporting customer, delegate, admin, and super-admin roles within a single Flutter application. Quantified operational outcomes were not provided.

## Technology

Flutter, Dart, Firebase, and role-based access control.
